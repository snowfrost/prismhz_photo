# 新编写法示例

以下是文本示例，尚未用它们生成新图。每条默认 MJ 独立包含已指定的 profile；显式关闭示例单独标明。

## 1. 他眼中的她：车站等我

构图：第一视角站在她几步外，中景，女生偏右，车站向左延伸；她刚抬眼发现观看者。

```text
First-person standing eye-level medium shot from a few steps away at a quiet Japanese suburban station. One young woman stands at frame right beneath the platform shelter, visible from head to below her waist. She lifts her eyes toward the viewer and begins to smile, one hand still holding the strap of her dark school bag. Long loose black hair falls below her shoulders, with a few fine strands lifted by the breeze. A white short-sleeved sailor blouse with a navy collar. The empty platform extends toward the left; a softly blurred shelter post gives foreground depth without obscuring her. Warm late-afternoon side light touches her cheek and hair against deep olive foliage and cool teal platform shade. Natural warm skin, creamy white fabric, gentle highlight bloom, readable eyes and extremely subtle grain. Shunji Iwai-inspired youth-film atmosphere, the small relief of seeing someone arrive. --no text, watermark, collage, selfie, coarse grain --ar 16:9 --profile e1u2l58 --v 8.2
```

中文释义：平视第一视角，从几步外看向安静的日本郊区站台。长发女生在画面右侧，从头部到腰部以下入镜；她握着书包带，刚抬头发现你，笑意开始出现。左侧站台延伸，虚化柱子提供前景。暖侧光落在脸颊与发丝，深绿树木和冷青阴影衬托奶白制服。自然肤质、柔和高光、极轻颗粒。

已加入的负面词：文字、水印、拼贴、自拍、粗颗粒 / text, watermark, collage, selfie, coarse grain。

## 2. 双人相处：河堤聊天，竖版

构图：两位成年男大学生在纵向画面里略作前后错位，从头到膝入镜，注意力在对话上。

```text
Eye-level medium shot of exactly two adult male university students seated side by side on low riverbank steps, framed from head to knees in a vertical composition. The nearer student sits slightly lower at frame left, holding a closed notebook on his lap. The other sits a little farther back at frame right, turning toward his friend mid-conversation with an easy smile. Both have short dark hair and relaxed everyday clothing, an ivory cotton shirt and a pale blue shirt. Their hands remain separate and their gazes stay within the conversation. River water and a narrow band of distant trees recede behind them; the step edges create depth below. Soft warm evening light, deep olive greens, cool teal shade, natural warm skin and restrained highlight bloom. Quiet Shunji Iwai-inspired everyday youth-film atmosphere, extremely subtle grain. --no text, watermark, collage, coarse grain --ar 3:4 --profile e1u2l58 --v 8.2
```

中文释义：两位短发成年男大学生并肩坐在河堤台阶上，竖版中景从头到膝。左侧一人更近、更低，合上的笔记本放在腿上；右侧一人稍后，转头和朋友说话。两人不看镜头，手的位置清楚。暖夕光、深绿树木、冷青阴影和柔和肤色形成日常亲近感。

已加入的负面词：文字、水印、拼贴、粗颗粒 / text, watermark, collage, coarse grain。

## 3. 空教室：明确要求纯文字版

本例用户明确要求“没有人物，不用风格码”，所以没有 profile。还需关闭网页中可能开启的自动 Personalization。

```text
Eye-level wide shot through the open doorway of an empty Japanese classroom after school. Worn wooden desks recede toward tall side windows; one pale curtain lifts slightly in the breeze. A closed notebook and a small folded paper star remain on the nearest desk. The room contains no people. Warm late-afternoon window light crosses the tabletops and floor, with a deep olive chalkboard and cool teal shade in the farther corners. Creamy whites, soft amber wood, calm shadow detail, gentle highlight bloom and extremely subtle grain. Layered foreground doorway and quiet background space, Shunji Iwai-inspired youth-film atmosphere, the stillness after a shared afternoon. --no people, human silhouettes, text, watermark, collage, coarse grain --ar 16:9 --v 8.2
```

中文释义：从门口平视空教室，木桌椅向侧窗退去，浅色窗帘轻动。最近的桌上留下合上的笔记本和纸星。窗光横过桌面与地面，远处为深绿黑板、冷青阴影。用空间与物件表现散场后的安静。

已加入的负面词：人物、人影、文字、水印、拼贴、粗颗粒 / people, human silhouettes, text, watermark, collage, coarse grain。

## 4. 用户明确要求 ImageGen：站台同一场景

生成一张16:9横幅写实青春电影感画面。镜头代表站在站台上的同伴，以自然站姿视线从几步外看她。画面右侧只有一名长发女生，从头部到腰部以下入镜；她穿白色短袖水手服、海军蓝领口，手还握着深色书包带，刚抬眼看见镜头外的同伴，嘴角开始笑起来。脸和手都清楚可见。左侧留出向远处延伸的安静站台，前景一根虚化棚柱不遮脸。傍晚暖侧光照到一侧脸颊和发丝，背景树木呈深橄榄绿，站台阴影偏冷青。肤色自然温暖，白布柔润，高光轻柔，暗部有细节，颗粒极轻。保留日常抓拍的姿态。避免自拍角度、额外人物、文字、水印、拼贴、粗颗粒和塑料皮肤。

这是独立编写的中文版本；没有 MJ 参数，也没有声称复现 profile 的全部效果。
